Who I help

Care built around you, not your diagnosis

These are the conditions I see most often.

Stroke

Getting back to daily tasks after a stroke — dressing, cooking, bathing, and moving safely around your home.

Brain injury

Rebuilding routines and habits after a traumatic or acquired brain injury, at a pace that works for you.

Spinal cord injury

Adapting how you do everyday things, and finding positioning that protects your skin and comfort.

Parkinson's & MS

Managing energy, movement, and safety as things change over time.

Seating & mobility

For anyone whose posture, positioning, or wheelchair fit is not working the way it should.

Seating & mobility evaluations

A certified look at your wheelchair, cushion, and positioning — for comfort, skin safety, and the paperwork that funding requires.

About seating & mobility
Image —
There is no better place to practice a task than where you will actually do it.

A clinic gym is useful. But it is not your kitchen, your bathroom, or your front steps. Working in your own home means we deal with the real obstacles in your day.

It also means family or caregivers can join a session whenever that helps.

How a visit works

Credentials

One therapist, start to finish

Every visit and every plan comes from the same person, who knows your history.

RESNA ATP

Assistive Technology Professional

A certification in choosing and fitting equipment — wheelchairs, cushions, and the tools that make daily tasks possible.

RESNA SMS

Seating and Mobility Specialist

A further certification specific to wheelchair seating and positioning, and the clinical documentation funders ask for.

Private pay

No insurance paperwork or session caps

The plan follows your goals rather than what a policy will authorise.
