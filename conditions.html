Who I help

Care built around you, not your diagnosis

Every plan starts with the person in front of me. These are the conditions I see most often — but if yours is not listed, it is still worth asking.

Stroke

After a stroke, everyday tasks can feel different or unsafe. We work on what you want to get back to, in the place you do it.

What we might work on

  • Dressing, bathing, and grooming with one working side
  • Safe transfers to bed, chair, toilet, and car
  • Using your affected arm and hand again
  • Cooking and kitchen safety
  • Setting up your home so it works for you

Brain injury

A traumatic or acquired brain injury can change memory, attention, and energy. We rebuild routines step by step, at a pace that does not wear you out.

What we might work on

  • Daily routines and memory strategies
  • Managing fatigue through the day
  • Reducing noise, light, and clutter at home
  • Getting back to cooking, chores, and hobbies
  • Building habits that stick

Spinal cord injury

After a spinal cord injury, the goal is doing what matters in a new way — and protecting your body while you do it.

What we might work on

  • New ways to dress, bathe, and groom
  • Transfer technique and equipment
  • Pressure relief and skin protection
  • Positioning in your chair and your bed
  • Equipment that fits how you actually live

Parkinson's & MS

With a progressive condition, needs change over time. We plan for how things are now, and stay ready for what comes next.

What we might work on

  • Saving energy across the whole day
  • Movement and motor planning strategies
  • Fall prevention at home
  • Tremor and fine motor tasks
  • Adjusting the plan as things change

Seating & mobility

If your chair hurts, if you slide forward, or if your skin is breaking down, your setup is not right. That can be measured and fixed.

What we might work on

  • Full wheelchair and cushion assessment
  • Pressure injury risk review
  • Posture and positioning corrections
  • Matching equipment to your goals
  • Documentation for funding
