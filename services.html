Services

What working together looks like

Two services, both delivered in your home. No clinic, no waiting room, no rushing to a gym across town.

Neurologic OT

In-home occupational therapy

One-on-one sessions built around the tasks that matter most in your own home — dressing, bathing, cooking, transfers, and home safety. The small routines that add up to independence.

Because we work where you live, what we practice transfers directly. No clinic gym standing in for real life.

What is included

  • A full evaluation of your goals, your home, and how you move through it
  • A written plan you keep, in plain language
  • Hands-on sessions in your own space
  • Equipment and home setup recommendations
  • Training for family or caregivers, when you want it
RESNA ATP & SMS

Seating & mobility evaluations

A certified assessment of your wheelchair, cushion, and positioning needs. The goal is lower pressure injury risk, better comfort, and paperwork that actually supports your funding request.

Whether you are due for a new evaluation or something about your current setup is not working, this is a focused look at your equipment and how it fits your body.

What is included

  • Measurements, posture assessment, and pressure mapping review
  • Trial of cushions and positioning components where possible
  • Clear equipment recommendations with reasons
  • Documentation written for funding review
  • Follow-up once equipment arrives

How it works

What a first visit looks like

  1. We talk first

    A short call, no charge. You tell me what is going on and what you want to get back to. I tell you honestly if I can help.

  2. I come to you

    The first visit is an evaluation in your home. We walk through your day, your space, and your goals together. Usually about 90 minutes.

  3. You get a plan

    A written plan in plain language — what we will work on, in what order, and roughly how long. You keep it, and we adjust it as we go.

Image —
There is no better place to practice a task than where you will actually do it.

Clinic gyms are useful, but they are not your kitchen, your bathroom, or your front steps. Working at home means therapy deals with the real obstacles in your day.

It also means the people who live with you can be part of it, which often matters more than anything I do in a single session.

Ready to start?

Tell me what you need and I will follow up to see if we are a good fit.
